# Robot makes a PB & J

## Assumptions

- We have a fully charged robot with empty hands
- There is a countertop with the following items:
    - Jar of PB
    - Jar of jelly
    - Loaf of wrapped bread
    - Butter knife
    - Plate
    - Napkin

## Steps to follow
1. Unwrap the bread, remove two slices of bread from the wrapper, place the 2 bread on the plate side by side to each other.
2. Open the jar of PB & Jelly, afterwards place the lid down on the counter.
3. Pick up the knife in one hand and the open PB in the other hand.
4. Dip the knife in the PB to scoop the PB, afterwards set down the jar.
5. Pick up a slice of bread and spread PB over the flat surface using the knife.
6. Put the bread with the PB on it back on the plate. clean side down.
7. Pick up the napkin and wip excess PB from the knife, set down the napkin after the knife is clean.
8. Pick up the jelly, open the jar afterwards put the lid down on the counter, pick up the knife and use it to scoop the jelly, set down the jar.
9. Pick up bread that does not have PB on it.
10. spread the jelly on the bread that you have picked up on the flat surface.
11. Set down the jelly bread on the other bread that have PB on it, jelly slide down.
12. Pick up the lids and put the lids back on the correct jars, pick up the napkin and toss it, then put the ingredients and the knife used for making the PB & J away.

## Exit conditions
- Constructed sandwich and plate are the only items on the table.
